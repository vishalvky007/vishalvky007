The problem

Most delivery time is spent waiting, not building.

38% of sprint capacity goes to reworkDefects found after handoff, not during development.
11 days median lead timeFrom merged pull request to production release.
4.2 handoffs per changeEach one a queue, an approval and a context reset.
What we hear Teams rarely lack talent or intent. They lack a release path they trust, so every change gets batched, and every batch gets riskier. Cadence is a symptom; the constraint sits in the path to production.

How we engage

Three phases, each with a decision gate.

Phase 01

Diagnose

2 weeks · 2 consultants
  • Value stream map, commit to production
  • DORA baseline from your own telemetry
  • Costed backlog of constraints
Phase 02

Stabilise

6–8 weeks · embedded pod
  • Trunk-based flow and test strategy
  • Pipeline rebuilt, environments on demand
  • Release runbook the team owns
Phase 03

Scale

Quarterly · advisory
  • Second and third team onboarded
  • Platform standards and golden paths
  • Handover, then we step back
